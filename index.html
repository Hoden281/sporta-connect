About Us

We're Rethinking How Athletes Connect

Finding people to play with shouldn't be hard. Sporta Connect brings together players, organizers, and local communities so you always have a game to join and people to play with.

Whether you're a weekend warrior or a seasoned competitor, we're building tools that make your sporting life easier.

  • Find local games — discover pickup games and organized leagues near you
  • Build your team — recruit players for any sport, any skill level
  • Track & compete — stats, rankings, and match history in one place
  • Community first — sports are better together
